## 2. Explicitly Labeled Assumptions (SYNTHETIC)
* **Powertrains & Charging:** Individual equipment electric powertrains, battery capacities (e.g., 80 kWh capacities), charging station power limits (e.g., 20 kW chargers), and energy tariffs are **synthetic assumptions** created solely to demonstrate optimization mechanics in the prototype[span_9](start_span)[span_9](end_span). 
* **Operational Integration:** We are not connected to, nor do we control, actual Port of Gulfport machinery or live utility databases[span_10](start_span)[span_10](end_span). All calculated cost/energy savings are estimates.
